# DentaFlow SaaS - Clinic User Manual

**Welcome to DentaFlow!**

This guide will help you get started with DentaFlow and make the most of its features. If you have any questions, please contact our support team at support@dentaflow.ai.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1. Logging In

1.  Go to [https://app.dentaflow.ai](https://app.dentaflow.ai)
2.  Enter the email and password provided to you by your clinic administrator.
3.  Click "Login".

If you have forgotten your password, click the "Forgot Password?" link and follow the instructions.

### 1.2. The Dashboard

The dashboard is the first screen you see after logging in. It gives you a quick overview of your day:

-   **Today's Appointments:** A list of your appointments for the day.
-   **Key Metrics:** Number of patients, appointments, and new conversations.
-   **Recent Activity:** A feed of recent patient interactions.

## 2. Managing Patients

### 2.1. Searching for Patients

1.  Click on the "Patients" tab in the left-hand menu.
2.  Use the search bar at the top to search by name, phone number, or email.

### 2.2. Viewing Patient Details

Click on a patient's name in the search results to view their details, including:

-   Contact information
-   Appointment history
-   Medical records
-   Conversation history

### 2.3. Adding a New Patient

1.  Click on the "Patients" tab.
2.  Click the "Add Patient" button in the top right.
3.  Fill in the patient's details and click "Save".

## 3. Managing Appointments

### 3.1. Viewing the Calendar

1.  Click on the "Calendar" tab.
2.  You can view the calendar by day, week, or month.
3.  Use the arrows to navigate to different dates.

### 3.2. Booking a New Appointment

1.  Click on an empty time slot in the calendar.
2.  A dialog box will appear. Search for the patient.
3.  Select the appointment type and duration.
4.  Add any notes.
5.  Click "Save".

An automated confirmation SMS will be sent to the patient.

### 3.3. Rescheduling or Canceling

1.  Click on the appointment in the calendar.
2.  Click "Edit" to change the time or date.
3.  Click "Cancel" to cancel the appointment.

The patient will be notified of any changes.

## 4. AI Patient Communication

DentaFlow's AI agents help you communicate with patients 24/7.

### 4.1. How it Works

-   **Alex (Receptionist):** Handles appointment booking, rescheduling, and general inquiries.
-   **Sarah (Dental Assistant):** Answers questions about treatments and post-op care.

Patients can communicate with the AI via SMS. All conversations are visible in the patient's profile.

### 4.2. Viewing Conversations

1.  Go to a patient's profile.
2.  Click on the "Conversations" tab.
3.  You can see the full history of the patient's interaction with the AI.

### 4.3. Manual Handoff

If the AI cannot handle a request, it will flag the conversation for manual review. You will see a notification on your dashboard. You can then take over the conversation and reply to the patient directly.

## 5. Clinic Administration (for Admins)

If you are a clinic administrator, you will have access to additional settings.

### 5.1. Managing Staff

1.  Go to "Settings" > "Staff".
2.  Here you can add, edit, or remove staff members.
3.  You can assign roles (Admin, Doctor, Staff) to control their permissions.

### 5.2. Managing Billing

1.  Go to "Settings" > "Billing".
2.  Here you can:
    -   View your current subscription plan.
    -   Update your payment method.
    -   View and download past invoices.

### 5.3. Clinic Settings

1.  Go to "Settings" > "Clinic".
2.  Here you can update:
    -   Clinic name and contact information.
    -   Working hours.
    -   Appointment types and durations.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes. DentaFlow is fully HIPAA compliant. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We take your data security very seriously.

**Q: Can I import my existing patient data?**
A: Yes. Please contact our support team, and we will assist you with the data import process.

**Q: What happens at the end of my 30-day free trial?**
A: We will contact you to see if you would like to continue with a paid plan. If you choose not to, you can export all of your data.

**Q: How do I get support?**
A: You can email us at support@dentaflow.ai or use the live chat feature in the app.
