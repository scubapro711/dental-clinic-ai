## Action Required

A Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with Twilio is required to ensure HIPAA-compliant use of their SMS services for patient communication.

**Steps:**

1.  **Locate BAA:** Access Twilio's legal portal to find their standard BAA.
2.  **Review:** Have legal counsel review the terms of the BAA.
3.  **Sign:** Execute the BAA through Twilio's required process.
4.  **Store:** Save a copy of the executed BAA in this directory.

**Note:** DentaFlow cannot send PHI via SMS until this BAA is in place. All SMS communications must be limited to non-PHI content (e.g., appointment reminders without specific health details) until this is resolved.
