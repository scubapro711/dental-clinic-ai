# Research Findings: Chatbot Limitations in Healthcare

**Source:** Chaudhry & Debi (2024) - "User perceptions and experiences of an AI-driven conversational agent for mental health support"
**Citation:** mHealth. 2024 Jul 5;10:22. doi: 10.21037/mhealth-23-55
**URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11304096/

---

## Key Finding: "AI limitations detract from the user experience"

45 user comments discussed 3 critical limitations of AI chatbots:

### 1. Understanding and Contextualization Issues (n=26)

**The Problem:**
- Chatbots fail to grasp **context** of conversations
- Programmed to identify **trigger words** but ignore context
- Offer generic relief regardless of relevance

**User Quotes:**
> "The AI often doesn't grasp what's happening in our conversations. It appears to be programmed to identify trigger words (anxious, overwhelmed, depression, etc.) and offer relief for those symptoms, regardless of their relevance to the conversation's context." (July 4, 2023)

> "I downloaded it for my paranoid schizophrenia, but it was of no help. The AI's failure to understand and tailor its responses to my specific circumstances has only worsened my mental health, making this app unsuitable for me." (August 17, 2023)

> "I was seeking guidance on establishing a detailed plan for my goals, but Wysa seemed overwhelmed and unable to assist me through the intricacies of my situation." (August 17, 2021)

**Impact:**
- Frustration among users
- Misinterpretations
- Generic answers that fail to meet actual needs
- Can be **detrimental** to mental health

---

### 2. Redundancy and Predictability (n=14)

**The Problem:**
- Chatbot responses are **repetitive**
- Rigid, structured patterns
- Becomes less helpful over time

**User Quotes:**
> "And while I like telling them [my health issues] out, and having a listening ear, you need to work on Wysa's ability to adapt in conversation, because a lot of what it says is repetitive and that can trigger a feeling of not being heard." (December 6, 2022)

> "I just had an incredibly unhelpful and frustrating 'conversation' with Wysa where it parroted back at me to demonstrate my 'progress' with the situation. I ended up much worse off than when I began." (August 5, 2021)

> "Also, after a few months' usage, the AI starts to sound very redundant, and doesn't help anymore." (February 18, 2020)

**Impact:**
- Feeling of **not being heard**
- Can make situation **worse**
- Lack of engagement over time
- Dissatisfaction during long-term usage

---

### 3. Limited Conversational Flow (n=13)

**The Problem:**
- Cannot reciprocate like a human
- Relies on **scripts**
- Limited follow-up capability
- Constrains depth and flexibility

**User Quotes:**
> "The chat function has no actual interaction ability. No matter what I say to it, the chatbot seems to have its own topic that it is stuck trying to discuss." (November 7, 2023)

> "It's painfully robotic sometimes and sometimes answers don't align with what you request." (May 1, 2023)

**Impact:**
- Feels **mechanical and impersonal**
- Breaks flow of conversation
- Diminishes sense of engagement
- Users yearn for dynamic dialogue
