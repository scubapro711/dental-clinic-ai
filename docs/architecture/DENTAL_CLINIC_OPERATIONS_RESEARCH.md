## 📅 APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING

### Standard Appointment Durations

| Procedure Type | Duration | Scheduling Notes |
|----------------|----------|------------------|
| **Routine Checkup + Cleaning** | 30-60 min | Most common, schedule in morning |
| **New Patient Visit** | 60-90 min | Includes medical history, X-rays, exam |
| **Simple Filling** | 30-60 min | Can be scattered throughout day |
| **Root Canal** | 60-90 min | Complex, needs dedicated time block |
| **Crown Preparation** | 60-120 min | First visit, needs follow-up |
| **Crown Placement** | 30-60 min | Second visit |
| **Extraction (Simple)** | 30-45 min | Quick procedure |
| **Extraction (Surgical)** | 45-90 min | Complex, needs more time |
| **Dental Implant** | 60-120 min | Surgical procedure |
| **Orthodontic Initial** | 60-120 min | Braces placement |
| **Orthodontic Adjustment** | 15-30 min | Quick follow-up |
| **X-rays Only** | 15-30 min | Can be added to other appointments |
| **Emergency Appointment** | 30-60 min | Keep 1-2 slots open daily |

### Best Practices for Scheduling

#### 1. **Time Blocking Strategy**
- **Morning (8:00-12:00):** High-production procedures (crowns, implants, complex cases)
- **Afternoon (13:00-17:00):** Routine cleanings, quick procedures
- **Emergency Buffer:** 30-minute window daily (usually late morning)

#### 2. **Block Scheduling (Cluster Scheduling)**
- Group similar procedures together
- Improves resource management
- Reduces setup/teardown time
- Example: All cleanings on Monday/Wednesday mornings

#### 3. **Appointment Intervals**
- **Standard:** 30-minute blocks
- **Some practices:** 10-15 minute intervals for flexibility
- **Buffer time:** 10 minutes between complex procedures

#### 4. **Scheduling Strategies**
- **Morning appointments:** Schedule backward from noon
- **Afternoon appointments:** Schedule forward from 1 PM
- **Don't schedule more than 30-45 days out** (reduces cancellations)

### No-Show & Cancellation Management

**Industry Statistics:**
- **Missed appointments cost:** $200/hour
- **70% of patients** say wait times affect experience
- **No-show rate:** 5-10% is normal, >15% is problematic

**Best Practices:**
1. **Automated Reminders**
   - Send 48 hours before appointment
   - Use SMS, Email, or Phone
   - Include appointment details + preparation instructions
   - Allow easy confirm/reschedule

2. **Cancellation Policy**
   - Require 24-48 hours notice
   - No-show fee: $50-75
   - Communicate clearly at booking
   - Enforce consistently

3. **Waitlist Management**
   - Keep active waitlist for last-minute openings
   - Auto-notify when slots become available
   - Reduces revenue loss from cancellations

- Sterilization and infection control
- Patient preparation
- Inventory management

- Appointment scheduling
- Patient check-in/check-out
- Insurance verification and billing
- Phone management

## 📊 CLINICAL WORKFLOW

### Patient Journey

1. **Appointment Booking**
   - Phone, online, or walk-in
   - Collect basic info
   - Send confirmation

2. **Pre-Appointment**
   - Send reminder (48 hours before)
   - Send forms (new patients)
   - Verify insurance

3. **Check-In**
   - Arrive 10-15 minutes early
   - Complete/update forms
   - Verify insurance and payment method

4. **Clinical Visit**
   - Medical history review
   - Examination
   - X-rays (if needed)
   - Diagnosis
   - Treatment plan discussion
   - Procedure (if same-day)

5. **Check-Out**
   - Schedule follow-up
   - Process payment
   - Provide post-care instructions
   - Answer questions

6. **Follow-Up**
   - Post-treatment check-in (24-48 hours)
   - Review request (if positive experience)
   - Next appointment reminder

### Medical Documentation

**Required Records:**
- Patient demographics
- Medical history (allergies, medications, conditions)
- Dental history
- Treatment plans
- Progress notes
- X-rays and images
- Consent forms
- Insurance information
- Payment history

**Israeli Regulations:**
- Records must be kept for **7 years** (Ministry of Health)
- Electronic records acceptable
- Patient has right to access records
- Privacy protected under Patient Rights Law

## 🚨 MEDICAL SAFETY & ESCALATION

### AI Boundaries (Critical for DentaFlow)

**❌ AI MUST NEVER:**
- Provide diagnosis
- Prescribe medications
- Make clinical decisions
- Override dentist's judgment
- Give specific medical advice

**✅ AI CAN:**
- Provide general dental health information
- Schedule appointments
- Answer administrative questions
- Escalate urgent cases to dentist
- Remind about post-care instructions

### Escalation Protocol

**🔴 Level 1: EMERGENCY (Immediate)**
- Severe bleeding that won't stop
- Severe pain (10/10)
- Swelling affecting breathing
- Trauma with tooth loss
- Allergic reaction
- **Action:** Call emergency services, notify dentist immediately

**🟡 Level 2: URGENT (Same Day)**
- Moderate to severe pain (7-9/10)
- Swelling or abscess
- Broken tooth with pain
- Lost filling/crown with sensitivity
- **Action:** Offer same-day emergency appointment

**🟢 Level 3: ROUTINE**
- Mild discomfort
- Cosmetic concerns
- Routine checkup
- Follow-up appointment
- **Action:** Schedule regular appointment

## 🇮🇱 ISRAELI REGULATORY CONTEXT

### Ministry of Health Requirements

**Licensing:**
- Dentist must have valid Israeli license
- Clinic must be registered with Ministry of Health
- Regular inspections for hygiene and safety

**Privacy & Data:**
- Patient Rights Law (1996)
- Protection of Privacy Law (1981)
- Medical records retention: 7 years
- Electronic health records permitted

**Insurance:**
- Professional liability insurance required
- Clinic liability insurance recommended

### Kupot Holim (HMOs)

**4 Main HMOs:**
1. Clalit
2. Maccabi
3. Meuhedet
4. Leumit

**Dental Coverage:**
- Basic plan: NO dental for adults
- Supplementary plans: Partial coverage
- Children: Free preventive care (schools)
- Elderly (75+): Subsidized care
