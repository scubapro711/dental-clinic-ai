## 1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

DentaFlow Ltd. ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of your Protected Health Information (PHI). This Notice of Privacy Practices describes how we may use and disclose your PHI to carry out treatment, payment, or healthcare operations, and for other purposes permitted or required by law.

**Business Associate**: DentaFlow acts as a Business Associate under HIPAA on behalf of dental practices (Covered Entities).

---

## 2. What is Protected Health Information (PHI)?

PHI is information about you, including demographic information, that may identify you and relates to:
- Your past, present, or future physical or mental health or condition
- The provision of healthcare to you
- The past, present, or future payment for your healthcare

---

## 3. How We May Use and Disclose Your PHI

### 3.1 Uses and Disclosures for Treatment, Payment, and Healthcare Operations

We may use and disclose your PHI without your authorization for:

**Treatment**: Providing, coordinating, or managing healthcare and related services.
- Example: Sharing your treatment plan with your dentist through our platform

**Payment**: Billing and collecting payment for services.
- Example: Processing insurance claims and generating invoices

**Healthcare Operations**: Supporting the business activities of your dental practice.
- Example: Quality assessment, staff training, business planning

### 3.2 Uses and Disclosures That Require Your Authorization

We will obtain your written authorization before using or disclosing your PHI for purposes other than treatment, payment, and healthcare operations, except as described below.

You may revoke your authorization in writing at any time.

### 3.3 Uses and Disclosures That May Be Made Without Your Authorization

**Required by Law**: When required by federal, state, or local law.

**Public Health Activities**: To prevent or control disease, injury, or disability.

**Health Oversight Activities**: To authorized health oversight agencies for audits, investigations, or inspections.

**Judicial and Administrative Proceedings**: In response to a court order or lawfully issued subpoena.

**Law Enforcement**: To law enforcement officials for law enforcement purposes.

**Coroners, Medical Examiners, and Funeral Directors**: To identify a deceased person or determine cause of death.

**Organ and Tissue Donation**: To organizations involved in procurement, banking, or transplantation of organs.

**Research**: For research purposes when approved by an institutional review board.

**Serious Threat to Health or Safety**: To prevent or lessen a serious and imminent threat to health or safety.

**Military and Veterans**: If you are a member of the armed forces.

**National Security and Intelligence**: To authorized federal officials for intelligence and national security activities.

**Correctional Institutions**: If you are an inmate of a correctional institution.

**Workers' Compensation**: As authorized by workers' compensation laws.

## 4. Your Rights Regarding Your PHI

### 4.1 Right to Inspect and Copy

You have the right to inspect and obtain a copy of your PHI contained in a designated record set.

**How to Exercise**: Submit a written request to your dental practice.

**Timeframe**: We will respond within 30 days.

**Fees**: We may charge a reasonable, cost-based fee for copying and mailing.

**Denial**: We may deny your request in certain circumstances. You have the right to request a review of the denial.

### 4.2 Right to Amend

You have the right to request that we amend your PHI if you believe it is incorrect or incomplete.

**How to Exercise**: Submit a written request to your dental practice with a reason for the amendment.

**Timeframe**: We will respond within 60 days.

**Denial**: We may deny your request if the PHI:
- Was not created by us
- Is not part of the designated record set
- Is not available for inspection
- Is accurate and complete

### 4.3 Right to an Accounting of Disclosures

You have the right to receive an accounting of certain disclosures of your PHI made by us.

**Excluded Disclosures**:
- For treatment, payment, or healthcare operations
- To you or your personal representative
- Pursuant to your authorization
- For national security or intelligence purposes
- To correctional institutions or law enforcement officials
- As part of a limited data set

**How to Exercise**: Submit a written request to your dental practice.

**Timeframe**: We will respond within 60 days.

**Fees**: The first accounting in a 12-month period is free. We may charge a reasonable fee for additional requests.

### 4.4 Right to Request Restrictions

You have the right to request restrictions on certain uses and disclosures of your PHI.

**How to Exercise**: Submit a written request to your dental practice.

**Our Response**: We are not required to agree to your request, except:
- If you request restriction on disclosure to a health plan for payment or healthcare operations
- If the disclosure is not required by law
- If the PHI pertains solely to a healthcare item or service for which you have paid in full out-of-pocket

### 4.5 Right to Request Confidential Communications

You have the right to request that we communicate with you about your PHI by alternative means or at alternative locations.

**How to Exercise**: Submit a written request to your dental practice specifying how or where you wish to be contacted.

**Our Response**: We will accommodate reasonable requests.

### 4.6 Right to a Paper Copy of This Notice

You have the right to obtain a paper copy of this Notice, even if you have agreed to receive it electronically.

**How to Exercise**: Request a copy from your dental practice or download from our website.

### 4.7 Right to Notification of a Breach

You have the right to be notified if there is a breach of your unsecured PHI.

**Timeframe**: We will notify you without unreasonable delay and in no case later than 60 days after discovery of the breach.

## 5. Our Responsibilities

### 5.1 Safeguarding Your PHI

We are required by law to:
- Maintain the privacy and security of your PHI
- Provide you with this Notice of our legal duties and privacy practices
- Follow the terms of the Notice currently in effect
- Notify you if we are unable to agree to a requested restriction
- Accommodate reasonable requests for confidential communications

### 5.2 Security Measures

We implement appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your PHI:

**Administrative Safeguards**:
- Privacy and security policies and procedures
- Workforce training
- Business Associate Agreements

**Physical Safeguards**:
- Secure facilities and workstations
- Device and media controls

**Technical Safeguards**:
- Access controls (unique user IDs, passwords, 2FA)
- Encryption (TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest)
- Audit controls and monitoring
- Automatic logoff

### 5.3 Minimum Necessary

We will make reasonable efforts to use, disclose, and request only the minimum amount of PHI necessary to accomplish the intended purpose.

**Exception**: This does not apply to:
- Disclosures to healthcare providers for treatment
- Disclosures to you
- Uses or disclosures pursuant to your authorization
- Disclosures required by law
