> # HIPAA Technical Safeguards FAQs
> 
> This document provides answers to frequently asked questions about the HIPAA Security Rule's technical safeguards.
> 
> ## What are the HIPAA Technical Safeguards?
> 
> The HIPAA Security Rule's Technical Safeguards are a set of standards designed to protect electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI). These safeguards require covered entities to implement technical policies and procedures that allow only authorized persons to access ePHI. [1]
> 
> ## What are the Required and Addressable Implementation Specifications?
> 
> The Technical Safeguards are divided into required and addressable implementation specifications.
> 
> *   **Required:** These specifications must be implemented.
> *   **Addressable:** For these specifications, a covered entity must assess whether the specification is a reasonable and appropriate safeguard in its environment. If it is, the covered entity must implement it. If it is not, the covered entity must document why it is not and implement an equivalent alternative measure if reasonable and appropriate.
> 
> ## What are the Key Technical Safeguards?
> 
> | Safeguard | Description | Required/Addressable |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Access Control** | Implement technical policies and procedures for electronic information systems that maintain ePHI to allow access only to those persons or software programs that have been granted access rights. | Required |
> | **Unique User Identification** | Assign a unique name and/or number for identifying and tracking user identity. | Required |
> | **Emergency Access Procedure** | Establish (and implement as needed) procedures for obtaining necessary ePHI during an emergency. | Required |
> | **Automatic Logoff** | Implement electronic procedures that terminate an electronic session after a predetermined time of inactivity. | Addressable |
> | **Encryption and Decryption** | Implement a mechanism to encrypt and decrypt ePHI. | Addressable |
> | **Audit Controls** | Implement hardware, software, and/or procedural mechanisms that record and examine activity in information systems that contain or use ePHI. | Required |
> | **Integrity Controls** | Implement policies and procedures to protect ePHI from improper alteration or destruction. | Required |
> | **Person or Entity Authentication** | Implement procedures to verify that a person or entity seeking access to ePHI is the one claimed. | Required |
> | **Transmission Security** | Implement technical security measures to guard against unauthorized access to e-PHI that is being transmitted over an electronic communications network. | Required |
>
