# HIPAA Patient Rights FAQs

This document provides answers to frequently asked questions about patient rights under HIPAA.

## What are My Rights Regarding My Health Information?

The HIPAA Privacy Rule provides individuals with the right to:

*   Ask to see and get a copy of their health records.
*   Have corrections added to their health information.
*   Receive a notice that tells them how their health information may be used and shared.
*   Decide if they want to give their permission before their health information can be used or shared for certain purposes, such as for marketing.
*   Get a report on when and why their health information was shared for certain purposes.
*   If they believe their rights are being denied or their health information isn't being protected, they can file a complaint with their provider or health insurer, or with the U.S. government. [1]

## Can I Access My PHI in any Format?

Covered entities are required to provide individuals with access to their PHI in the form and format requested, if it is readily producible in that form and format. This includes providing electronic copies of PHI maintained electronically.

## How Much Can I be Charged for a Copy of My Records?

A covered entity may only charge a reasonable, cost-based fee for the labor and supplies associated with copying the records. The fee may not include costs associated with verification, documentation, or searching for and retrieving the PHI. [2]
