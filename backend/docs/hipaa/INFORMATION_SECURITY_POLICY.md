# DentaFlow - Information Security Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** October 8, 2025  
**Status:** Final

---

## 1. Purpose

This Information Security Policy outlines the framework of controls and procedures that DentaFlow Inc. ("DentaFlow") has implemented to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI) in compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) Security Rule.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all DentaFlow workforce members, including employees, contractors, and temporary staff, as well as all systems, networks, and data used to provide services to Covered Entities.

## 3. Policy

### 3.1. Risk Management (45 CFR § 164.308(a)(1))

DentaFlow shall conduct a periodic, accurate, and thorough assessment of the potential risks and vulnerabilities to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of ePHI held by DentaFlow. Risk analysis and management activities will be documented and reviewed at least annually.

### 3.2. Assigned Security Responsibility (45 CFR § 164.308(a)(2))

A Security Officer shall be designated to develop and implement the policies and procedures required by the Security Rule. The current Security Officer is [Name/Title to be assigned].

### 3.3. Workforce Security (45 CFR § 164.308(a)(3))

All workforce members with access to ePHI must undergo security awareness and training. Procedures for authorization, supervision, and termination of access will be implemented and enforced.

### 3.4. Access Control (45 CFR § 164.312(a))

Access to ePHI shall be limited to authorized individuals and based on the principle of minimum necessary use. Unique user identification, automatic logoff, encryption, and emergency access procedures are implemented.

### 3.5. Audit Controls (45 CFR § 164.312(b))

DentaFlow shall implement hardware, software, and/or procedural mechanisms that record and examine activity in information systems that contain or use ePHI. Audit logs are reviewed regularly for suspicious activity.

### 3.6. Integrity (45 CFR § 164.312(c)(1))

Policies and procedures shall be implemented to protect ePHI from improper alteration or destruction. Mechanisms shall be in place to corroborate that ePHI has not been altered or destroyed in an unauthorized manner.

### 3.7. Transmission Security (45 CFR § 164.312(e)(1))

ePHI transmitted over electronic networks shall be encrypted. DentaFlow uses TLS 1.3 for all data in transit.

### 3.8. Data Encryption (45 CFR § 164.312(a)(2)(iv))

All ePHI stored by DentaFlow ("at rest") is encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.

### 3.9. Business Associate Contracts (45 CFR § 164.308(b)(1))

DentaFlow will enter into a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with all Covered Entities and subcontractors that create, receive, maintain, or transmit ePHI on its behalf.

### 3.10. Incident Procedures (45 CFR § 164.308(a)(6))

DentaFlow has implemented an Incident Response Plan to address security incidents, including breaches of ePHI. All incidents will be documented, investigated, and mitigated.

### 3.11. Contingency Plan (45 CFR § 164.308(a)(7))

DentaFlow maintains a contingency plan, including data backup, disaster recovery, and emergency mode operation plans, to ensure the availability of ePHI.

## 4. Enforcement

Violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract, and may be subject to civil and criminal penalties as stipulated by HIPAA.

## 5. Review and Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed and updated at least annually or as needed to reflect changes in regulations, technology, or business operations.
