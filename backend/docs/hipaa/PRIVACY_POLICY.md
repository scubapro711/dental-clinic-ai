# DentaFlow - Privacy Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** October 8, 2025  
**Status:** Final

---

## 1. Purpose

This Privacy Policy outlines DentaFlow Inc.'s ("DentaFlow") commitment to protecting the privacy of Protected Health Information (PHI) in compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) Privacy Rule.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all uses and disclosures of PHI by DentaFlow's workforce members and systems in the course of providing services to Covered Entities.

## 3. Policy

### 3.1. Minimum Necessary Use and Disclosure

DentaFlow will make reasonable efforts to limit the use, disclosure of, and requests for PHI to the minimum necessary to accomplish the intended purpose.

### 3.2. Permitted Uses and Disclosures

DentaFlow will only use or disclose PHI as permitted or required by its Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with Covered Entities and as required by law. This is primarily for the purpose of providing the DentaFlow SaaS platform services.

### 3.3. Individual Rights

DentaFlow acknowledges the rights of individuals under the Privacy Rule and will assist Covered Entities in meeting their obligations to:

*   **Access:** Provide individuals with access to their PHI.
*   **Amendment:** Accommodate requests to amend PHI.
*   **Accounting of Disclosures:** Provide an accounting of disclosures of PHI.

All such requests from individuals will be directed to the respective Covered Entity.

### 3.4. Safeguards

DentaFlow will maintain appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the privacy of PHI, as detailed in the Information Security Policy.

### 3.5. Training

All workforce members with access to PHI will receive training on this Privacy Policy and the procedures necessary to protect the privacy of PHI.

### 3.6. Complaints

DentaFlow will investigate any complaints related to the privacy of PHI. Individuals may file complaints with the DentaFlow Privacy Officer or with the Secretary of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services.

### 3.7. No Retaliation

DentaFlow will not intimidate, threaten, coerce, discriminate against, or take other retaliatory action against any individual for the exercise of their rights under the Privacy Rule.

## 4. Privacy Officer

A Privacy Officer shall be designated to oversee the development, implementation, and maintenance of this policy. The current Privacy Officer is [Name/Title to be assigned].

## 5. Enforcement

Violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.

## 6. Review and Maintenance

This policy shall be reviewed and updated at least annually or as needed to reflect changes in regulations or business operations.
