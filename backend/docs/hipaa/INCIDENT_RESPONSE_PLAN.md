# DentaFlow - Incident Response Plan

**Version:** 1.1  
**Date:** October 18, 2025  
**Status:** Active

---

## 1. Purpose

This Incident Response Plan establishes the procedures to be followed in the event of a security incident affecting the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI) handled by DentaFlow Inc. ("DentaFlow"). The goal is to ensure a prompt, effective, and orderly response to security incidents, including breaches of unsecured ePHI.

## 2. Scope

This plan applies to all DentaFlow workforce members and all information systems that create, receive, maintain, or transmit ePHI.

## 4. Incident Response Phases

The incident response process is divided into four phases:

### 4.1. Phase 1: Identification and Reporting

Any workforce member who suspects a security incident must immediately report it to the Security Officer. An incident is defined as the attempted or successful unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or destruction of information or interference with system operations in an information system.

### 4.2. Phase 2: Containment, Eradication, and Recovery

*   **Containment:** The IRT will take immediate steps to contain the incident and prevent further damage or unauthorized access. This may include isolating affected systems, disabling compromised accounts, or blocking malicious IP addresses.
*   **Eradication:** The IRT will identify and eliminate the root cause of the incident. This may involve removing malware, patching vulnerabilities, or correcting misconfigurations.
*   **Recovery:** The IRT will restore systems to normal operation. This includes restoring data from backups, rebuilding systems, and verifying that the systems are secure before returning them to production.

### 4.3. Phase 3: Breach Assessment and Notification

If the incident involves ePHI, the Privacy Officer will conduct a risk assessment to determine if a breach of unsecured ePHI has occurred, in accordance with 45 CFR § 164.400-414.

If a breach is confirmed:

*   **Notification to Covered Entity:** DentaFlow will notify the affected Covered Entity(s) without unreasonable delay and in no case later than 60 days following the discovery of the breach.
*   **Notification to Individuals:** The Covered Entity is responsible for notifying affected individuals. DentaFlow will provide the Covered Entity with the necessary information to do so.
*   **Notification to HHS:** For breaches affecting 500 or more individuals, the Covered Entity must notify the Secretary of HHS. DentaFlow will assist the Covered Entity as required.

### 4.4. Phase 4: Post-Incident Activity

After the incident is resolved, the IRT will conduct a post-incident review to:

*   Document the incident and the response.
*   Identify lessons learned.
*   Update policies, procedures, and controls to prevent future incidents.

## 5. Documentation

All security incidents and actions taken will be documented in an incident tracking system. This documentation will be retained for at least six years, as required by HIPAA.

## 6. Training

All workforce members will receive training on this Incident Response Plan and their role in reporting and responding to security incidents.

## 7. Review and Maintenance

This plan shall be reviewed, tested, and updated at least annually or following a significant security incident.
